Where Satori came from: Mycroft, Vicki, and Dave

Built by Matthew Metcalf after watching people hit cost and waitlist walls. First came Mycroft (Stoic-only on Gemini: accurate, cold). Next came Vicki (Internal Family Systems for his wife Jamie: kind, private). A third prototype, Dave, tested gamified badges before public release was scoped strictly to adults 18+ only. Five experiments proved one law: warmth without structure breeds platitudes, and structure without warmth breeds a wall.

Frequently asked questions. Wisdom traditions, psychology, privacy, and cost.

How does Satori draw upon Stoicism (Epictetus & Marcus Aurelius) for anxiety?

Satori applies Epictetus's dichotomy of control and Marcus Aurelius's meditative reframing to separate what is in your sphere of agency from external events, pairing Stoic ataraxia and amor fati with human warmth.

How does Wu wei (non-forcing) and Zen non-attachment work in inquiry?

Satori introduces Wu wei (無為) from Laozi and Zhuangzi alongside Zen non-attachment (anicca) and the ensō circle, showing where you are forcing an outcome so you can act in alignment with reality.

What role do Advaita Vedanta, Hindu philosophy, and witness consciousness play?

Advaita Vedanta offers Sakshi (witness consciousness) alongside Bhagavad Gita karma yoga, helping you step back from fused identification with passing panic or fear of failure.

How does Satori integrate Ubuntu, Akan Sankofa, and Kemetic Ma'at?

Satori draws on Southern African Ubuntu (“I am because we are”) to heal isolation, Akan Sankofa ethics to retrieve forgotten wisdom from your past, and Kemetic Ma'at to ground truth and reciprocity.

How does Satori honor grief through Sufi contemplation (Rumi, Attar)?

Drawing on Rūmī's Masnavi and Attar's The Conference of the Birds, Satori uses Qalb (heart perception), Ishq (longing as connection), Fana & Baqa, and Tawakkul to honor grief as a furnace of renewal.

Does Satori replace therapy, counseling, or psychiatric treatment?

No. Satori works alongside therapy and is never a replacement for clinical care. It serves adults 18+, does not diagnose conditions, and in acute crisis immediately locks tools and routes to verified 24/7 human lifelines.

How does Satori use Internal Family Systems (IFS) to unblend parts?

Drawing on Richard Schwartz's IFS model, Satori treats harsh self-criticism as protective Manager parts shielding vulnerable Exiles, asking gentle questions that help you unblend and lead from Self.

How does Satori use ACT (defusion) and DBT (dialectical tensions)?

From Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Satori applies cognitive defusion to treat harsh thoughts as language events. From Marsha Linehan's DBT, it holds acceptance alongside commitment to change.

Can Satori help with developer burnout and imposter syndrome?

Yes. Precedence Rank 5 detects when coding prompts carry burnout or imposter pressure, solves the technical code problem completely first, and appends two grounded sentences without hijacking your terminal.

Why is Satori's Anti-Guru architecture different from self-help chatbots?

Satori is built to be outgrown. Success means needing it less over time. It offers at most one lens and one focused question per turn, avoiding 7-point homework checklists and artificial attachment.

How is privacy protected across CLI, web chat, and local models?

In CLI/IDE harnesses, Satori stores ≤500-char structural themes in a local SQLite vault at ~/.satori/memory/ (POSIX 0700, zero telemetry). In web chat, it stores nothing on disk while your chosen AI host processes the chat under its own policy.

What does Satori cost, and do I need a paid AI subscription?

Satori is free under Apache 2.0. Custom zip uploads on Claude.ai or ChatGPT Workspaces may require a paid plan from those hosts, or you can use Satori free via Gemini Skills, pasting compact/SKILL.md, or Ollama.
